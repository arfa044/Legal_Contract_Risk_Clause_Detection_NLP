SAMPLE SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. Payment Terms. The customer shall pay each invoice within thirty days of receipt. Any overdue amount may incur a late fee of five percent per month.

2. Termination. The provider may terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason, without prior notice to the customer.

3. Limitation of Liability. The provider's liability under this agreement shall be unlimited for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential loss.

4. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect confidential information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except as required by law.

5. Indemnity. The customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the provider from all claims, losses, damages, costs, and expenses arising out of use of the services.

6. Notices. All notices must be made in writing and delivered to the contact address provided by each party.

7. Governing Law. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction stated in the order form.
